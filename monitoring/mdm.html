Capabilities and privacy

The sidecar runs non-root with dropped capabilities and a read-only root filesystem. Its service binds only to pod loopback, without publishing a host port. It has no container socket, host filesystem or SIM-authentication configuration mounted. No authentication keys, raw UE logs, GUTI/SUCI, GPS or packet payloads are displayed.

Prometheus-compatible MDM metrics · History is bounded to the latest 120 collected samples and resets with the dashboard.
